ABOUT & ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

A peek into my technical background, mindset, and what drives my work:

Why Non-Zero-Sum Software?

Zero-sum thinking assumes that for one entity to gain, another must lose. In computing and software architecture, this manifests as proprietary lock-in, fragmented data silos, and friction. Non-zero-sum engineering focuses on open standards, collaborative workflows, and AI tooling that elevates developer productivity and user autonomy simultaneously.

My Background & Focus

I am a versatile software engineer with hands-on experience spanning frontend reactivity, backend microservices, and modern AI engineering. From engineering healthcare platforms like DentAI to crafting robust game loops in C# .NET and pushing code daily in LeetCode 365, I obsess over code clarity, testability, and resilient architecture.

AI Agent Systems & Context Engineering

In modern systems engineering, LLMs alone are just engines; the differentiator is deterministic workflow control, context engineering, and state management. Through LangGraph, multi-agent evaluation frameworks, and MCP (Model Context Protocol), I design agents that execute complex multi-step reasoning with verifiable correctness.
