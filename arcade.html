THE ARCADE

NON-ZERO-SUM LAB

#

interactive game theory simulations & system experiments

In game theory, a non-zero-sum game is a situation where the total winnings and losses can be greater than zero. In this lab, test how cooperative strategies consistently outperform selfish algorithms in repeated interactions.
